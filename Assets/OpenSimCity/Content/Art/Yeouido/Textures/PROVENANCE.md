# Yeouido v05 packed material maps

Source: `Seoul_Yeouido_v05/Korea_City_Assets_v05.blend` from the user-authored Blender source package.

These maps were supplied inside the user's self-contained Blender asset library.
`ArtSource/Blender/export_yeouido_textures.py` extracts the packed 2K base-color,
OpenGL normal and roughness maps without saving or changing the source `.blend`.
They remain first-party OpenSimCity art inputs; no third-party license is inferred.

Selected surfaces: asphalt, concrete, granite, plaster, bark, timber and soil.
